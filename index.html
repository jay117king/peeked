The Problem

Most goal apps treat humans like machines

Long to-do lists ignore how the brain actually works. The result? Overwhelm, abandonment, and another abandoned New Year’s resolution.

🧠

Working memory limits ignored

Humans can only hold a handful of items in working memory. Giant task lists exceed cognitive capacity and create anxiety instead of clarity.

📉

No dopamine strategy

Motivation is driven by progress signals. Traditional apps give almost no intermediate wins, so the brain loses interest long before the finish line.

⚡

One-size-fits-none

Everyone gets the same rigid structure. People with ADHD, varying energy patterns, or different learning styles are left behind.

The Solution

Pathbreak: Science meets game design

We combine peer-reviewed research on how brains actually complete goals with a progressive, low-pressure game layer.

What makes Pathbreak different

  • Neurologically optimized step sizes — Micro-steps sized to working-memory limits and ultradian rhythms (typically 15–45 minutes).
  • Evidence-based sequencing — Uses goal-gradient effect, implementation intentions, spaced practice, and deliberate practice principles.
  • Adaptive by design — Continuously recalibrates based on your completion data, energy reports, and uploaded materials.
  • Game layer without toxicity — XP, skill trees, flexible streaks, and narrative framing that motivate without shame.
  • Built for real brains — First-class support for ADHD and executive-function challenges using clinical guidelines.

How It Works

From ambition to daily quests in minutes

01

Enter your goal

Type anything: “Read the Bible”, “Understand Java”, “Run a half-marathon”, “Launch a side project”. Pathbreak immediately offers intelligent refinements.

02

Answer a few smart questions

Adaptive questionnaire covers timeframe, current skill level, preferred learning style, available time, energy peaks, motivation style, and any constraints (ADHD, anxiety, resources).

03

Receive your personalized roadmap

The AI generates a hierarchical plan: major milestones → weekly themes → daily micro-steps (15–45 min) with clear success criteria and optional resources.

04

Play, adapt, and level up

Complete quests, earn XP, watch your skill tree grow. The system continuously adjusts step size and recovery based on real progress and energy data.

The Science

Built on real research, not vibes

Pathbreak’s core engine draws from decades of peer-reviewed work in how humans learn, stay motivated, and complete goals.

Cognitive Neuroscience

  • Working memory capacity limits (Cowan, Baddeley)
  • Attention residual and task-switching costs
  • Dopamine-driven motivation loops
  • Ultradian rhythms and optimal focus blocks

Behavioral Psychology

  • Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer)
  • Goal-gradient effect (Hull, Kivetz)
  • Habit stacking and environment design
  • Self-determination theory (autonomy, competence, relatedness)

Learning & Productivity Science

  • Deliberate practice principles
  • Spaced repetition and interleaving
  • Desirable difficulties
  • Progress monitoring and feedback loops

Clinical & Executive Function

  • ADHD-friendly task design guidelines
  • Burnout prevention and recovery research
  • Anxiety-sensitive goal framing
  • Energy-based scheduling approaches

The Decomposition Engine

1

Goal Understanding

LLM + structured extraction normalizes the goal, detects domain, implied scope, and constraints.

Contextual Profiling

Combines questionnaire answers with historical completion data and energy patterns.

Knowledge Retrieval

RAG over a curated scientific corpus + any user-uploaded documents or media.

Hierarchical Decomposition
