The problem

Most goal apps ignore the brain

Lists grow. Attention doesn’t. Overwhelm wins.

🧠

Working memory overload

We hold only a few items in mind. Giant lists exceed capacity and create anxiety instead of clarity.

📉

No recovery design

Motivation needs intermediate wins and regulated arousal. Apps rarely train breath or memory alongside tasks.

⚡

One-size-fits-none

Rigid structures fail ADHD, shifting energy, and different learning styles.

The solution

Science meets clarity

Evidence-based step design, plus mind-training tools that support follow-through.

  • Neurologically sized steps — 15–45 minutes, aligned with working memory and focus rhythms.
  • Memory exercises — Spaced retrieval and light drills woven into learning paths.
  • Breathing protocols — Short, research-linked regulation before hard blocks.
  • Adaptive plans — Shrink steps and add recovery when completion drops.
  • Transparent science — Citations for every major design choice.

Mind training

Memory

Spaced retrieval & light drills

For skill and knowledge goals (languages, Java, exams), Pathbreak can insert short retrieval practice: free recall prompts, interleaving, and spaced review instead of re-reading only.

  • Active recall after micro-lessons
  • Spaced review scheduled from your path
  • Interleaved practice across related topics
Research

Cepeda et al. (2006) — distributed practice. Kang (2016) — spaced repetition. Bjork & Bjork (2011) — desirable difficulties.

Breathing

Regulation before hard focus

Before dense work blocks, Pathbreak can offer brief, guided breathing — a practical tool to lower cognitive noise and support sustained attention.

  • Short box or paced breathing (1–3 minutes)
  • Optional pre-focus and post-focus resets
  • Skip anytime — never required to progress
Research

Zaccaro et al. (2018) — breath-control and brain/autonomic states (open access). Balban et al. (2023) — brief structured respiration. Always optional; not medical treatment.
