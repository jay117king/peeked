Memory

Spaced retrieval & light drills

For skill and knowledge goals (languages, Java, exams), Pathbreak can insert short retrieval practice: free recall prompts, interleaving, and spaced review instead of re-reading only.

  • Active recall after micro-lessons
  • Spaced review scheduled from your path
  • Interleaved practice across related topics
Research

Cepeda et al. (2006) — distributed practice improves long-term retention vs massed practice. Kang (2016) — spaced repetition is efficient for durable learning. Bjork & Bjork (2011) — desirable difficulties (including retrieval) strengthen memory.

Breathing

Regulation before hard focus

Before dense work blocks, Pathbreak can offer brief, guided breathing — not as wellness fluff, but as a practical tool to lower cognitive noise and support sustained attention.

  • Short box or paced breathing (1–3 minutes)
  • Optional pre-focus and post-focus resets
  • Skip anytime — never required to progress
Research

Zaccaro et al. (2018) — systematic review: slow breathing linked to autonomic and psychological changes relevant to calm focus. Balban et al. (2023) — brief structured breathwork can influence mood and physiology in controlled settings. Always optional; not medical treatment.
